# Typing Chicken Privacy Policy

Effective November 24, 2026.

Typing Chicken does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

- To react to your typing, the app uses the macOS Input Monitoring permission. It only reads which key was just pressed, on your Mac, to choose the short label shown in the animation. Keystrokes are never saved, logged, or transmitted.
- The app makes no network connections and contains no analytics, advertising, or tracking.
- Your settings (size, position, click-through) are stored only on your Mac.

Contact: https://github.com/jo9900/typing-chicken-support/issues
